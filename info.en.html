Compare current aerial images from the Geoportal Berlin with earlier years and historical maps. Split the screen and choose a map for each half – they all move and zoom together.

There are aerial images from almost 100 years, the map of Berlin 1:5000, maps of the urban development of Berlin since 1650 and other historical maps, like Straube's overview plan or the geological map. For example, you can see how badly the buildings were damaged in 1945, what stood where the Hauptbahnhof is today or what your neighbourhood looked like a hundred years ago.

A project of the OK Lab Berlin. The tiles are served by tiles.codefor.de and the source code is on GitHub: maps.berlin.codefor.de and @jochenklar/maps. See also luftbilder.berlin.codefor.de.
